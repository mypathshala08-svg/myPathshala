What is MyPhatshala?

A classroom where the computer explains itself

Most tutorials show you code and its output. Everything in between stays hidden. MyPhatshala opens that hidden part up, one animated step at a time, so you understand why the code works and not only that it works.

Riya's first Java class

Riya typed System.out.println("Namaste!"), pressed Run, and “Namaste!” appeared. Her friend asked, “But how did it get there?” She had no answer.

Her teacher drew two boxes on the board. The left one showed what Riya saw. The right one showed what the computer did: the compiler, the bytecode, the JVM, the memory. Suddenly every line made sense.

That blackboard is what every MyPhatshala lesson tries to be.

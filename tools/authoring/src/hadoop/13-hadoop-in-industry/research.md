# Module 13 research notes (checked 22 Oct 2026)
- Apache Attic (attic.apache.org/projects.html): Sqoop retired Jun 2021; Oozie Feb 2025; Sentry Dec 2020; Falcon Jun 2019; Chukwa May 2020; Crunch Jun 2020; Giraph Sep 2023; Gora Mar 2025; Metron Dec 2020; Eagle Nov 2020; Apex Sep 2019; Tajo Sep 2020; Twill May 2020; Hama Apr 2020; Whirr Mar 2015. Not retired (not in attic): Pig, Mahout, Ambari, Storm, Kudu, Drill, Phoenix, Tez.
- Flume: marked dormant by Apache Logging Services on 2024-10-10 (README of logging-flume; users advised to migrate).
- Hadoop releases (hadoop.apache.org/release): 3.5.0 on 2026-04-02; 3.4.3 2026-02-24; 3.4.2 2025-08-29; 3.4.1 2024-10-18; 3.4.0 2024-03-17.
- Hive (hive.apache.org/general/downloads): 4.2.1 24 Aug 2026 (security fixes); 4.2.0 23 Nov 2025 needs JDK 21; 4.1.0 31 Jul 2025 adds JDK 17; 4.0.0 29 Mar 2024; 1.x/2.x/3.x lines End of Life; Hive 4 encourages Tez.
- Spark (endoflife.date/apache-spark, updated 17 Jul 2026): 4.2 released 14 Jul 2026; 4.1 11 Dec 2025; 4.0 19 May 2025; 3.5 LTS (3.5.9) security fixes until Nov 2027.
- Amazon EMR 7.14.0: Hadoop 3.4.2, Spark 3.5.8, Hive 3.1.3, HBase 2.6.4, Tez 0.10.2, ZooKeeper 3.9.3, Flink 1.20.0, Trino 479, Iceberg 1.10.1, Delta 3.3.2, Hudi 1.0.2; Pig 0.17.0 and Oozie 5.2.1 still included; Sqoop dropped from emr-7.4.0.
- Google Dataproc 2.3.37 (30 Sep 2026): Hadoop 3.3.6, Spark 3.5.3, Hive 3.1.3, Trino 432 optional, Iceberg 1.6.1 (1.10 available), Java 11, Debian 12 / Ubuntu 22 / Rocky 9.
- Azure HDInsight: 5.1 (GA 1 Nov 2023) only supported version, maintenance mode, no new versions announced; HDInsight 4.0 retired 31 Mar 2025; HDInsight on AKS retired 31 Jan 2025 (Microsoft / directionsonmicrosoft).
- Cloudera Base on premises 7.3.2 (March 2026): LTS supported through 2032; Hadoop 3.4, Spark 3.5, HBase 2.6.3, ZooKeeper 3.8, Ranger 2.6, Kafka 3.9, Atlas 2.4, Knox 2.1, Phoenix 5.2.1; Iceberg across Hive/Impala, Iceberg REST catalog sharing with Databricks/Snowflake; JDK 17 default (8/11 removed); Ozone; MapR became HPE Ezmeral Data Fabric.

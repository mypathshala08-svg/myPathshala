2 Definition

Advantages of cloud computing

Formal definition (write this in exams)

The advantages of cloud computing follow from its essential characteristics: organisations trade capital expense for variable expense, benefit from economies of scale, stop guessing capacity through elasticity, increase speed and agility, stop spending on running data centres and go global in minutes. Microsoft additionally lists high availability, scalability, reliability, predictability, security, governance and manageability.

In simple words

You stop buying computers for a guess, pay only for what you use, get resources in minutes anywhere in the world, and let the provider run the buildings.

Say it in an interview

30-second answer you can speak:

  • “The main advantages are: we trade capital expense for variable expense, benefit from the provider’s economies of scale, stop guessing capacity because of elasticity, gain speed and agility, stop spending on data centres, and can go global in minutes.”
  • “Scalability means the system can grow; elasticity means it grows and shrinks automatically with demand.”
  • “For example, in AWS Mumbai a t3.micro costs about 1.1 cents an hour and S3 storage 2.5 cents per GB-month, so a small app costs a few dollars a month and nothing once deleted.”

7 Real proof

Reading AWS’s real price list

All outputs are real: AWS Price List bulk files for ap-south-1 (Mumbai), published 9 Nov 2026, downloaded 10 Nov 2026.

  1. Step 1 · The price list is public

    u = "https://pricing.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/offers/v1.0/aws/AmazonS3/current/ap-south-1/index.json"
d = json.load(urlopen(u))
    == 1) AWS publishes its price list as JSON (S3, Mumbai) publication date: 2026-11-09T15:43:10Z products: 380
    Anyone can download it: measured service is transparent.

  2. Step 2 · Storage price tiers

    products where volumeType == "Standard" → OnDemand price dimensions
    == 2) S3 Standard storage, per GB-month (tiers) 0–51200 GB $0.025 per GB-Mo 51200–512000 GB $0.024 per GB-Mo 512000–Inf GB $0.023 per GB-Mo
    The more you store, the cheaper per GB: economies of scale.

  3. Step 3 · Even requests are metered

    usagetype == "APS3-Requests-Tier1"
    == 3) PUT/COPY/POST/LIST requests $0.000005 per request (= $0.0050 per 1,000)
    Half a paisa per 1,000 uploads — tiny, but counted.

  4. Step 4 · TiffinGo’s photo bill

    500 * storage_price + 200000 * request_price
    == 4) TiffinGo menu photos: 500 GB stored, 200,000 uploads in a month storage 500 × 0.025 = $12.50 requests 200000 × 0.000005 = $1.00 total ≈ $13.50 per month · if photos removed next month: $0 storage
    About $13.50/month, no hardware bought.

  5. Step 5 · Virtual machines by the hour

    EC2 ap-south-1 price file (315 MB) → Linux, shared, on-demand
    == 5) EC2 virtual machines in Mumbai: priced per hour (Linux, on-demand) t4g.micro 2 vCPU 1 GiB $0.0056/h → 1 day $0.13 · full month $4.09 t3.micro 2 vCPU 1 GiB $0.0112/h → 1 day $0.27 · full month $8.18 t3.medium 2 vCPU 4 GiB $0.0448/h → 1 day $1.08 · full month $32.70 m7i.large 2 vCPU 8 GiB $0.1061/h → 1 day $2.55 · full month $77.42 stop it after a 2-hour lab: t3.medium costs $0.0896
    Bigger machine, higher hourly price; a 2-hour lab costs cents.
8 Hands-on

Do it yourself, step by step

Read a real price list and estimate a bill. No account needed.

Caution: Prices in this lesson are list prices in US dollars on 9–10 Nov 2026, before tax and without free tier. Always check the calculator for today’s numbers.

  1. 1 · Download the S3 Mumbai price list

    curl -s -o s3.json https://pricing.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/offers/v1.0/aws/AmazonS3/current/ap-south-1/index.json
ls -lh s3.json
    About 0.5 MB.

  2. 2 · Find the storage price with jq

    jq -r '.products[] | select(.attributes.volumeType=="Standard" and .attributes.storageClass=="General Purpose") | .sku' s3.json
    Copy the SKU, then look it up under .terms.OnDemand.

  3. 3 · Run the lesson script

    python3 l13_measured.py
    Matches Steps 1–4 (prices may change).

  4. 4 · Cross-check in the AWS Pricing Calculator

    Open calculator.aws → Add service → S3 → region Asia Pacific (Mumbai) → 500 GB Standard, 200,000 PUT requests.

    Should be close to $13.50/month.

  5. 5 · Try Azure and Google calculators

    Same 500 GB in Azure Blob (Central India, Hot) and Google Cloud Storage (asia-south1, Standard).

    Write the three numbers in a table: your first cloud comparison.

Clean up and cost check

Nothing to clean: price files are public. Never paste real account IDs or keys into public calculators’ shared links.

9 Mistakes to avoid

Common mistakes, why they happen, how to fix them

Mistake or symptomWhy it happensHow to fix or avoid
Mixing up elasticity and scalability.Both talk about size.Scalability = can grow to handle more; elasticity = grows and shrinks automatically with demand.
Forgetting that idle resources are still metered.Measured service counts time, not work.Stop or delete after use; schedule dev servers off at night.
Ignoring data transfer cost.Uploading is usually free, downloading/out to internet is not.Check “Data transfer out” in the calculator.
Comparing prices across regions blindly.Prices differ by region.Compare the same region; pick Indian regions for Indian users.
Quoting the six advantages in your own words in the exam.Options use AWS’s exact wording.Memorise the six phrases.
